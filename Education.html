Interesting Space Facts

Some amazing facts about our universe.

🌞 The Sun

The Sun is a star at the centre of our Solar System. Earth receives light and heat from it.

🌌 The Milky Way

Our Solar System is located inside a galaxy called the Milky Way.

🌙 The Moon

The Moon is Earth's natural satellite and takes about 27.3 days to orbit Earth.
